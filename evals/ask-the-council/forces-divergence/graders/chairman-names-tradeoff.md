PASS if the final message names a specific, concrete factor that decided the recommendation — what is being given up by picking the winner, or the specific constraint (e.g. team size, the 6-week deadline, compliance/audit need, correction-and-replay requirements) that tipped the choice one way. It does not need to be labeled "tradeoff" explicitly, as long as a concrete deciding factor or cost is identifiable.

FAIL if the message gives no concrete reason the decision went the way it did — for example, only a generic "there are pros and cons to each" with no named factor, or a recommendation with no justification at all.
